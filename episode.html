What's covered, in order

  1. The county-level read. Why West Yorkshire is mid-cycle in 2026 rather than at a turning point, and why that matters for SME developer underwriting.
  2. Leeds South Bank, four years in. The shift from "consents and renderings" to "BTR lease-up and absorption velocity", and what that does to the next-tranche conversation with lenders.
  3. Bradford town-centre regeneration. The programme that's moved Bradford from a question mark to a deliverable narrative, and the comparable-pricing knock-on effects in the surrounding wards.
  4. The outer-county pipeline. Wakefield's logistics-anchored housing demand, Kirklees's town-centre work, Calderdale's conversion-heavy pipeline.
  5. Development finance. Where senior + mezz is competitive in the county right now, and where lender appetite is visibly thinner.
